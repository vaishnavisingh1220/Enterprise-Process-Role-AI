# Demo Guide

## Objective

Demonstrate how Process-to-Role Intelligence dynamically creates, analyzes, stores, and queries enterprise knowledge in real time.

Estimated duration: **2–3 minutes**

---

# Step 1 – Explore an Existing Role

Select an existing role from the Role Dashboard.

Show:

- Activities
- AI Impact
- Confidence
- AI Impact Distribution
- Executive AI Report

Explain that the platform models enterprise knowledge using the relationship:

```
Process → Activity → Role → AI Impact
```

---

# Step 2 – Create New Enterprise Knowledge

Open the **Live Knowledge Ingestion** panel.

Enter:

Role

```
AI Ethics Officer
```

Process

```
AI Governance
```

Activity

```
AI Model Bias Auditing
```

Description

```
Review AI model outputs to identify, document, and mitigate algorithmic bias before deployment.
```

Click **Analyze Live**.

Explain:

- Live research is performed.
- AI impact is generated.
- New entities are persisted.
- Knowledge graph updates immediately.

---

# Step 3 – View Full Role Analysis

Click **View Full Role Analysis**.

Highlight:

- New role appears instantly.
- Dashboard updates without restarting.
- AI Readiness metrics are generated.
- Executive AI Report is available.

---

# Step 4 – Generate Executive AI Report

Click **Generate Executive AI Report**.

Explain that:

- Deterministic reasoning gathers enterprise evidence.
- The LLM converts structured reasoning into executive language.

---

# Step 5 – Enterprise Knowledge Assistant

Open the assistant.

Example questions:

- How will AI change the AI Ethics Officer role?
- Compare Procurement Manager with AI Ethics Officer.
- Which roles have the highest automation potential?
- What new responsibilities are emerging because of AI?

Explain that responses are grounded in the enterprise knowledge graph rather than relying solely on an LLM.

---

# Key Takeaways

- Dynamic enterprise knowledge creation
- Explainable AI analysis
- Persistent knowledge graph
- Executive AI reporting
- Enterprise conversational assistant

The demonstration shows the complete lifecycle from data ingestion to AI-powered decision support.
